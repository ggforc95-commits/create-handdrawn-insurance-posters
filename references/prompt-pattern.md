# Prompt Pattern

Use this structure for each separate poster.

```text
Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset type: Chinese insurance social-media poster, image <N> of <TOTAL> in one unified series.

Input images:
- Image 1..N: style references only. Extract oversized handwritten Chinese,
  paper background, marker annotations, illustration density, and composition.
  Do not copy their subject matter, branding, or slogans.

Primary request:
Create one <ASPECT_RATIO> poster for this exact copy:
"<LINE 1>"
"<LINE 2>"
"<LINE 3>"

Typography and hierarchy:
- Render the quoted Chinese verbatim and highly legibly.
- Use oversized bold black handwritten Chinese.
- <CIRCLE_PHRASE> receives a rough <COLOR> marker circle.
- <UNDERLINE_PHRASE> receives a rough <COLOR> marker underline.

Illustration:
<ONE CLEAR NARRATIVE SCENE THAT VISUALIZES THE COPY>

Series constants:
- warm white/off-white paper background;
- black, red, yellow, and a small amount of blue;
- simple friendly hand-drawn cartoon;
- spacious, high-contrast composition;
- same stroke, character, and annotation treatment as the rest of the series.

Constraints:
- correct readable Chinese;
- preserve punctuation and wording;
- no extra marketing claims;
- no invented prices or benefits;
- no logo, watermark, garbled text, or photorealism;
- no graphic injury or frightening scene.
```

## Scene Mapping Heuristics

| Copy intent | Prefer |
|---|---|
| Multiple protections | Policy sheet plus shield and a few labeled benefit icons |
| Low daily cost | Small coin doodles plus a protected child/family scene |
| Campus and off-campus | One protection device spanning school and home/road |
| Independent student life | Dorm or campus desk with laptop, medicine kit, sports gear, and a concerned student |
| Accident or claim story | Mild incident, helper, bandage, policy card, and reassuring shield |

Do not add all possible symbols. Select the smallest set that makes the message immediate.
